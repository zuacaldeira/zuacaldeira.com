2 · Lead plus list

For: the ICE 2026 paper comes first and large, with Theorem 15 beside it and a filled button, like the Software page's lead. The other three sit under "Earlier texts".

Costs: the theorem repeats the home and Software bands, and it is a sentence under a text (decision 4a said none). The page is 338 px longer. The lead has to be moved by hand when a newer text arrives.

Why 1: the page's job is to be the complete, honest list, and the theorem already has two homes (the home page and the Software page). Option 1 stays true to decision 4a and needs no change when the first write-up arrives.
