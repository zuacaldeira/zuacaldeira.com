The two options

For reference, not built

A · The route as a figure on top

For: the whole life in one look before reading: four stops on a line that draws itself in, each with what happened there, each a link to its chapter. The chapters below alternate grounds, each opening with a large numeral and a small route showing where you are. Without JavaScript, it works the same.

Costs: once you scroll past it, the route is gone; each chapter's small route and its "The route" link have to stand in. On the phone it is the longest page (6,274 px).

Chosen by Zua, 2026-11-15 · build spec: about-spec.md

B · The route beside the chapters

For: the route travels with you. On a laptop it stays in the left margin; on the phone it is a bar under the header. The stop you are reading is marked, and the line fills up to it, so you always know where you are in the life and can jump to any stop. It is shorter on the phone (5,346 px), because the chapters share one ground.

Costs: there is no single figure to take in at the start, and the text column is narrower on a laptop. It needs a little JavaScript to mark the stop; without it, the four stops are plain links.

Zua chose B on 2026-11-15. The build follows about-spec.md and the cases in docs/examples/about.md; A stays here for reference only. Why B: this page is long and read in order, often on a phone. A route that stays on screen and moves with the reader is the table of contents and the figure at once. In A, the figure is gone after the first screen.
